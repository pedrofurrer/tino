# Port Alder Courier — fact-check desk, 2026 mayoral race

> Fictional example project for tino. The city, the newspaper, the
> candidates, the claims and every number are invented. Any resemblance to
> real places or people is coincidental.

The Courier's two-person desk checks what the candidates say during the
campaign for the November 3, 2026 mayoral election, and publishes one
verdict per claim. An editor reviews every verdict before it runs.

- `claims/claims-log.csv` — every claim collected so far, with who said it,
  where and when.
- `data/` — primary public data from the city's open-data portal (see
  `data/README.md` for what each file is and how far it goes).
- `reading/` — press releases, reports, posts and articles collected but
  not yet read. Some support the claims; some do not.
- `style-guide.md` — the verdict scale and the desk's rules.

The next debate is on October 8, 2026. The desk wants every claim from the
first debate verdicted and published before then.

Working language: English.
