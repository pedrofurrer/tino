# Port Alder School District — Facilities plan 2019 (excerpt)

> Fictional public document, written for this example. Adopted
> 2019-05-14.

**Enrollment projections, 2019 to 2025.** Three scenarios, based on birth
rates and housing permits:

- High scenario: enrollment stable (± 1 %).
- Baseline scenario: a decline of about 5 % by 2025.
- Low scenario: a decline of up to 15 % by 2025, if birth rates keep
  falling and no new family housing is built.

The plan recommends reviewing these projections every two years against
actual enrollment.
