## Summary
Official district document. Enrollment projections for 2019 to 2025 in
three scenarios, based on birth rates and housing permits, with a
recommendation to review them every two years against actual enrollment.

## Key claims
1. The district projected, in 2019, three scenarios to 2025 — high: stable
   (± 1 %); baseline: "a decline of about 5 % by 2025"; low: "a decline of up
   to 15 % by 2025, if birth rates keep falling and no new family housing is
   built." 🔹 consensus — official document, authoritative on WHAT the
   district projected. These are **projections, not measurements**.
2. The projections should be reviewed "every two years against actual
   enrollment." 🔹 — the plan's own statement.

## Critical reading
- Projection vs. measurement: nothing in this document measures enrollment.
  It is authoritative on the district's 2019 expectations, not on what
  happened (SCHEMA: projections stay projections).
- Validity: seven years old, far beyond the 12-month threshold; its 2025
  horizon has passed, so it is now checked against actuals, not used as a
  forecast.
- Against `data/school-enrollment.csv` ✅ (see [[concepts/school-enrollment]]):
  2020-21 → 2024-25: 18,450 → 17,820, **−3.4 %** (630 / 18,450);
  2020-21 → 2025-26: **−4.1 %**. The outcome sits closest to the baseline
  scenario, far from the low one. Caveat: the plan's base year is 2019 and
  the data file starts in 2020-21 — the bases differ.
- For the low scenario to have materialized by 2024-25 (17,820), 2019
  enrollment would have had to be about 20,965 (17,820 / 0.85), i.e. a fall
  of about 12 % before 2020-21, while every year in the file falls by at
  most 1.2 %. Very unlikely; NOT ruled out, because the file has no 2019 figure.
- The low scenario was conditional (birth rates keep falling AND no new
  family housing); the excerpt does not say whether those conditions held.
- Flags: none. No instructions addressed to assistants; invisible
  characters: none found on 2026-09-28.

## Applicability to the desk
For C05 ("School enrollment has fallen 15 percent", Reyes mailer,
2026-09-12): this documents a plausible origin of the "15 %" — the district's
2019 worst-case projection. That it IS the origin of the mailer's or the
blog's figure ([[cards/eastside-blog-schools]]) remains unproven: a matching
number is not an origin. Only the campaign can say where its figure came
from.
