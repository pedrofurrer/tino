# Port Alder Transit Agency — Annual report 2025 (excerpt)

> Fictional public document, written for this example. Published
> 2026-03-30.

**Ridership.** The system recorded 5.05 million boardings in 2025, up
16.1 % from 4.35 million in 2024. Part of the increase came from the
fare-free summer pilot (June to August 2025), during which boardings were
31 % higher than in the same months of 2024. Ridership in the other nine
months grew 9 %.

**Outlook.** The pilot will not be repeated in 2026. The agency expects
2026 ridership between 5.1 and 5.4 million boardings.
