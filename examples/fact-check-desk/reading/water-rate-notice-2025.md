# Port Alder Water Utility — Rate notice (2025)

> Fictional public document, written for this example. Mailed to customers
> in November 2024.

To fund the replacement of lead service lines, the city council approved
the following residential rates:

| Effective | Fixed monthly charge | Rate per 1,000 gallons |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 (current) | $12.00 | $5.10 |
| January 2025 | $15.00 | $5.45 |
| January 2026 | $18.00 | $5.72 |

A household using 5,000 gallons a month will see its bill change from
$37.50 in 2024 to $42.25 in 2025 and $46.60 in 2026.
