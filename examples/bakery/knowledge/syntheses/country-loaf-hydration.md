# Country loaf hydration — what we believe today

Updated: 2026-09-28

## Current recommendation
Keep **72 %** (720 g water per 1 kg flour) — ✅ validated with the PREVIOUS
flour. With the new mill's flour (from 2026-09-04) it is an **open
question**: no change without our own trial. Any change is the owner's
decision (ARCHITECTURE invariant 1 and 4).

## Evidence
- ✅ Bake log, previous flour, batches of 12 loaves:
  75 % (2026-06-12) → 2 of 12 too flat to sell;
  80 % (2026-06-19) → 5 of 12 flat, too slack to shape by hand at our pace;
  72 % (2026-06-26) → 0 of 12 rejected.
- 🔸 The new supplier advises up to 70 % for hearth loaves
  ([[cards/flour-mill-datasheet-2026-09]]).
- 🔸 One impression of a weaker dough with the new flour (bake log
  2026-09-04); see [[concepts/new-mill-flour]].
- Rejected source: a blog post pushing 80 % (triage 2026-09-28, LOG) —
  opinion without data, already answered by the 80 % trial above.

## What would change it
- Rejects per batch at 72 % with the new flour: if flat loaves reappear, a
  side-by-side trial at 70 % vs 72 % (same day, same oven) decides.
- A new datasheet or a new supplier: re-open.
