# Los Robles Bakery — recipes and sales analysis

> Fictional example project for tino. The bakery, its numbers and its
> sources are invented.

A small neighborhood bakery: two people and a wood-fired oven. The owner
works with an AI agent on two fronts:

1. **Recipes** in Markdown (one per file in `recipes/`), to write down what
   today lives only in the baker's head.
2. **Sales analysis**: `data/sales-2026.csv` holds weekly sales per product
   from June to September 2026, and `analysis/sales_summary.py` summarizes
   units and revenue per month.

`notes/bake-log.md` is the baker's notebook of test bakes. `reading/` holds
articles and supplier documents the owner has collected and not yet read.

Working language: English.
